Goal

Collect early leads for SILKROAD and understand how users behave on our landing page: which products they view, whether they add items to cart, and where they drop off in registration.

1) Lead collection section (Newsletter)

We added a newsletter subscription section on the landing page: “Get early access to new drops”. Visitors can leave an email (required) and phone number (optional) to receive new arrivals, launch updates, and an early-bird discount.

  • CTA: “Subscribe & get 10% off”
  • Collected data: email (required), phone (optional)
  • Tool: Formspree (form submissions)

2) Analytics setup (PostHog)

We use PostHog for Web analytics (visitors/pageviews), Product analytics (conversion funnels), and Session replay (observe real user sessions and friction points).

Tracked events

  • Lead: lead_form_started, lead_submitted
  • Account: register_attempt, user_registered, user_verified, user_logged_in, user_logged_out
  • Products: product_viewed, product_added_to_cart (optional: checkout_started, purchase_completed)

Funnels

  • Lead funnel: $pageview → lead_form_started → lead_submitted
  • Shop funnel: product_viewed → product_added_to_cart → user_registered (→ checkout_started)

Learnings & next steps

  • Improve the registration UX (reduce steps, clearer validation messages).
  • Add trust signals (shipping/returns, secure checkout) near CTA.
  • Test different CTA offers (10% off vs free shipping vs early access).
  • Use session replays to identify the biggest drop-off point and fix it.
